**题目 1**

我选题目 1。以下是我的作文：

*In my opinion, universities should require students to take courses outside their major. This is because it can help students to broaden their knowledge. Nowadays, many students only focus on their own major and don't know anything else. If a student take a course outside major, they can learn new things and meet different people.*

## 重点句修改

❌ *If a student take a course outside major, they can learn new things and meet different people.*
✅ *When a student takes a course outside their major — a history elective for a computer science major, for instance — they are exposed to frameworks and questions that their core curriculum rarely addresses.*

❌ *This will help him understand the world better.*
✅ *This exposure encourages students to approach problems from perspectives their major alone does not provide.*

❌ *Universities should make these courses easy to pass so students don't feel too much pressure.*
✅ *Rather than making such courses easier, universities could integrate them into the credit system so they complement, rather than compete with, major requirements.*

## 高分改写版

Should universities require students to take courses outside their major? I believe they should, not because general knowledge is inherently valuable, but because the most interesting problems rarely fit within a single discipline.

When a computer science student takes a course in urban history, for instance, they are not merely "broadening their knowledge." They are learning that the algorithms they write may shape how cities allocate resources — a connection neither discipline teaches alone. This kind of cross-pollination is precisely what the modern workplace demands. Employers increasingly report that they value graduates who can translate insights across fields rather than those with narrow technical proficiency alone.

That said, simply adding requirements without adjusting the system would burden already stretched students. The solution is not to pile on extra credits but to redesign existing ones — for example, by offering team-taught courses where a data analysis project is assessed jointly by the statistics and sociology departments.

The point is not to make students study everything. It is to ensure they leave university understanding that what they know is always partial — and that the gaps between disciplines are where real insight often begins.

## 个性化模板

### 保分模板（稳定版）
```
[观点] I believe that [topic] is [position], not because [reason1], but because [reason2].
[论证] When [specific group] engages with [specific scenario], they [specific outcome].
[让步] Admittedly, [counterargument]. However, this concern can be addressed by [specific solution].
[结论] Ultimately, the value of [topic] lies not in [shallow benefit], but in [deeper insight].
```

### 提分模板（灵活版）
```
[现象切入，不直接表态] Ask a [typical student] whether [topic], and they will probably say [assumption]. The reality is more complicated.
[具体场景论证] Consider [specific example]. What looks like [surface] turns out to be [deeper truth].
[驳论] The objection that [counterargument] has some force, but it mistakes [A] for [B].
[收束，不重复前文] The test of [policy/topic] should be whether it makes students [specific outcome], not whether it [shallow metric].
```
